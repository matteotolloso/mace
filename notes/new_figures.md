**Common protocol (all generated figures).** Five dataset splits; each metric is computed within a split and summarized as mean ± 95% Student-t interval (df = 4). Test configurations whose smallest interatomic distance falls below the smallest one in that split's training data are excluded for every model and signal. This rule reads geometry only. It removes 13–16 of 5000 Energy-OOD configurations per split and essentially nothing elsewhere, and replaces the 0.5% total-variance trim, which selected on the uncertainty being evaluated. Because of both changes, numbers in the current text will change, most visibly in the system split. Details and reproduction commands: `new_figures/README.md`.
